## 🚀 Featured Translational Projects

### 1. 📊 [Cancer AI Survival Prediction Model](https://github.com/taiwo-oluwamojo/TCGA-Survival-Analysis-ML)
*Machine Learning Framework for Clinical & Genomic Survival Risk Stratification*
- **Overview:** Built an end-to-end Python pipeline to predict patient survival outcomes using multidimensional clinical and multi-omic datasets from TCGA.
- **Methods:** Implemented feature engineering, missing value imputation, and ensemble supervised algorithms (Random Forest, SVM). Evaluated performance using C-index and Kaplan-Meier risk stratification curves.
- **Key Outcome:** Achieved robust cross-validated risk stratification, identifying high-risk biomarker sub-clusters for clinical decision support.
- **Tech Stack:** Python, `scikit-learn`, `lifelines`, `matplotlib`, `seaborn`

---

### 2. 🧬 [Pan-Cancer Genomic Pipeline: TP53 Alteration Profiling](https://github.com/taiwo-oluwamojo)
*Comparative Multi-Omic Analysis Across Human & Comparative Oncology Cohorts*
- **Overview:** Designed a reproducible bioinformatic workflow to profile *TP53* mutation prevalence, copy number alterations (CNAs), and downstream gene expression impact across major human and animal cancer types.
- **Methods:** Automated data retrieval and normalization from cBioPortal and TCGA APIs; performed differential expression and variant classification analyses.
- **Key Outcome:** Standardized automated visualization of mutation hot-spots and survival impact, demonstrating translational utility across species.
- **Tech Stack:** Python, REST APIs, cBioPortal, R, Bioconductor

---

### 3. 🛡️ [Structural Immunoinformatics & Epitope Design Pipeline](https://github.com/taiwo-oluwamojo)
*In Silico Vaccine Candidate Screening & MHC-Binding Analytics*
- **Overview:** Conducted multi-step immunoinformatics screening to identify conserved, non-allergenic, high-affinity B-cell and T-cell epitopes for target pathogens.
- **Methods:** Executed sequence alignment (MAFFT), antigenicity modeling (VaxiJen), 3D protein structure prediction (AlphaFold), and molecular docking simulations.
- **Key Outcome:** Identified top candidate multi-epitope constructs with high binding affinity for downstream wet-lab validation.
- **Tech Stack:** AlphaFold, MAFFT, VaxiJen, IEDB Tools, PyMOL
